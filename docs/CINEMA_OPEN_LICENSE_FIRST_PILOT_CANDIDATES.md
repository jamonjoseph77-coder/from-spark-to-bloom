# Cinema — First Open-Licence Content Candidates

**Cinema — Experience the World of Stories**

_Status: candidate diligence only. No file has been downloaded, uploaded, classified, tested or published by Cinema. The sources below publish Creative Commons permissions, but Cinema must preserve the exact source edition and credits and complete Ghana-specific checks before use._

## Why start here

Openly licensed films can provide a small, legitimate test catalogue while Cinema continues pursuing Ghanaian and African rights-holders. These works are not a substitute for local stories, and they do not create sponsor rights automatically.

A Creative Commons licence can permit commercial redistribution of the licensed work, subject to its conditions. It does not automatically clear trademarks, logos, separate music, third-party artwork, performer publicity/privacy rights, or a sponsor's use of a film or actor as an endorsement.

## Required attribution

Use the exact attribution requirements on the source page and preserve the original end credits.

- **Big Buck Bunny:** the source specifies Blender Foundation attribution and says the full credits roll must remain included when screening/broadcasting the whole film. Source: https://peach.blender.org/about/
- **Spring:** the source specifies “© Blender Foundation | cloud.blender.org/spring”. Source: https://studio.blender.org/projects/spring/pages/about/
- **Tears of Steel:** the source requires retaining the credit scroll. The source also notes that actors retain personal-image and privacy rights; do not use actor likenesses as commercial endorsements or sponsor creatives without separate permission. Source: https://mango.blender.org/about/

## What this does and does not permit

**Potentially permitted by the stated CC BY licences:** making the licensed film available, including through a commercial service, while meeting attribution and licence conditions.

**Not automatically permitted:** removing or changing credits; using excluded logos/trademarks; claiming Cinema made the film; using an actor's likeness to endorse a brand; treating the title as a sponsor asset; using separately licensed music/artwork outside the film; or assuming a different edition/file carries the same licence.

Do not add paid placements, sponsor naming, brand integrations or promotional edits to these titles until the specific proposed use has been reviewed against all relevant rights and restrictions. Keep the initial test content-only and clearly credited.

## Technical and legal intake checklist per title

- [ ] Open the primary source page and save a dated copy/screenshot of the licence statement.
- [ ] Identify the exact master/edition and verify that the licence covers that master.
- [ ] Obtain the master from the creator's official download source; do not use an unofficial mirror.
- [ ] Record file size, format, resolution, duration and checksum.
- [ ] Preserve original credits and add the required attribution on the Cinema title page.
- [ ] Check separate music, artwork, logos, performer and third-party material exclusions.
- [ ] Record licence version, source URL, access date and reviewer.
- [ ] Confirm Ghana NFA classification and platform licensing requirements before public exhibition.
- [ ] Test playback on mobile and desktop, including low-bandwidth conditions and seek/resume.
- [ ] Keep sponsorship/advertising permission marked “not granted” unless separately established.

## Priority recommendation

Use these three works as a controlled technical pilot only after the adult representative and qualified adviser confirm the proposed commercial streaming use and Ghana regulatory requirements. In parallel, keep the Ghanaian/African catalogue as the main acquisition priority. Do not count these films as published Cinema titles until the full acceptance checklist is complete.
