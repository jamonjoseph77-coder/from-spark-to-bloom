## 2. What Cinema is asking partners for

Cinema is seeking a limited initial catalogue for a digital entertainment platform, beginning with Ghana and potentially expanding to Nigeria. We are interested in feature films, series, documentaries, animation and other complete programmes.

Ask each partner to provide:
- A catalogue or title list, with synopsis, genre, language, runtime, year and available artwork.
- The legal rights holder for each title and the partner's authority to license it.
- Rights available: subscription-free/ad-supported/brand-sponsored or other permitted models, as negotiated; streaming/VOD; territory; language; exclusivity; term; devices; and whether embedding or hosting is permitted.
- Any restrictions on trailers, clips, subtitles, dubbing, edits, marketing and promotional stills.
- Commercial terms: upfront fee, minimum guarantee, revenue share, reporting, payment timing, taxes and any minimum commitments.
- Technical delivery: master files or authorised playback method, codecs/resolution, audio tracks, subtitles/captions, metadata, artwork, quality-control requirements and delivery schedule.
- Classification/certification status and any age-rating restrictions for intended territories.
- Contracting entity details and authorised signatory.

Do not assume that a producer owns all underlying rights. Verify music, screenplay/adaptation, performers, archive footage, artwork and other embedded material where relevant.

## 3. Preferred pilot structure to explore

Ask whether the partner would consider:
- A small pilot of 5–10 titles first.
- Non-exclusive, territory-limited rights where possible.
- No large upfront minimum guarantee, if commercially possible.
- A transparent revenue-share or fixed-fee model, negotiated title by title.
- A short initial term with clear renewal and takedown provisions.
- A written statement of rights and warranties for each title.

These are negotiation preferences only, not terms already agreed or guaranteed.

## 4. Qualification gates — no title goes live until all pass

1. **Identity:** legal name and contact of the rights holder / authorised licensor verified.
2. **Authority:** written evidence that the partner can grant the requested rights.
3. **Scope:** exact title/edition, territories, term, platform, streaming method, monetisation and exclusivity documented.
4. **Components:** music, performers, artwork, subtitles, clips and other embedded rights checked.
5. **Compliance:** Ghana/Nigeria classification, licensing and other applicable requirements reviewed with qualified adult/legal support.
6. **Agreement:** signed written licence and any required schedules completed by authorised adults.
7. **Delivery:** file or approved playback source received, metadata and subtitles checked, playback tested.
8. **Operations:** support contact, reporting, payment, expiry and takedown owner recorded.
9. **Publication:** separate internal approval after all prior gates pass.

A public trailer, YouTube upload, festival screening, public-domain claim, or catalogue listing is not proof that Cinema may host or commercially distribute the full title.

## 5. Ghana compliance checkpoint

The Ghana National Film Authority says VOD streaming platforms / film libraries are among the licence categories it regulates. It also describes separate producer, studio, distributor and exhibitor categories. Cinema's exact applicable category, eligibility, requirements and fees must be confirmed directly with the NFA before operating a Ghanaian VOD library.

Official information: https://nfa.gov.gh/apply-for-film-license/  
NFA contact shown on that page: licence@nfa.gov.gh / info@nfa.gov.gh.

Do not represent that Cinema is licensed until the appropriate licence is confirmed and issued.

## 6. Outreach sequence

- Day 0: send concise introduction and request the right contact / buyer access.
- After response: request catalogue and rights availability.
- Catalogue received: shortlist 5–10 pilot titles and complete title-level rights checks.
- Commercial discussion: compare cost, rights scope, delivery and reporting.
- Adult/legal review: review all contracts and compliance requirements.
- Signed agreement: receive authorised masters or playback access.
- Technical QA: test every title on mobile and desktop.
- Controlled pilot: publish only approved titles and monitor playback/support.

## 7. Pipeline status definitions

- **Lead:** organisation identified; no confirmed contact.
- **Contacted:** message sent; no response confirmed.
- **Responded:** human response received.
- **Catalogue received:** title list received; rights not yet cleared.
- **Rights review:** title-by-title authority and scope being checked.
- **Commercial discussion:** terms being negotiated.
- **Agreement review:** written agreement under adult/legal review.
- **Licensed:** signed rights grant verified.
- **Ingest / QA:** authorised content delivered and tested.
- **Approved to publish:** all legal, rights, technical and operational gates passed.
- **Live:** title published and playback confirmed.
- **Closed / declined:** no further action.
