# Cinema — Openly Licensed Film Pilot Slate

**Purpose:** identify complete short films that may be technically onboarded without negotiating a conventional title licence, because their official publishers expressly provide reuse/distribution permissions under Creative Commons licences.

**Important status:** These are *licence-evidenced candidates*, not Cinema-acquired or production-published titles. No files have been uploaded to Cinema. The exact file, attribution, edition, soundtrack and any separately excluded material must be checked before use. Adult review is required before ingestion or publication.

## Rights notes

- CC BY generally permits commercial reuse and redistribution subject to the licence conditions; preserve required creator credit, licence notice, source link and indication of modifications.
- Do not remove or crop out required end credits. Do not imply Blender Foundation endorses Cinema.
- Do not use project logos, studio marks or sponsor logos unless separately permitted.
- Tears of Steel project notes specifically caution that performers retain personal image and privacy rights for some uses of source footage. This slate concerns the finished film under its stated film licence, not reuse of raw footage or performer likenesses in advertisements.
- No separate sponsor campaign, endorsement, product placement, trailer edit or promotional derivative is included in this slate. Those require their own review.
- Ghanaian legal/regulatory classification and platform licensing obligations still need to be checked by an adult representative and qualified adviser before public release.

## Technical onboarding gate

For each candidate, record:
1. Exact official download URL, edition, container, resolution, frame rate, audio layout and file size.
2. File checksum and provenance.
3. Playback test on Cinema's current player, mobile browser and desktop browser; Smart TV test where available.
4. Audio/video sync, seeking, captions/subtitles if provided, aspect ratio and end-credit integrity.
5. Storage/hosting suitability and delivery performance. Do not upload large masters to GitHub.
6. On-screen attribution and full credit-roll preservation.
7. Adult approval of rights evidence and Ghana classification/licensing position.

## Proposed first pilot batch

Start with **Big Buck Bunny**, **Spring** and **Elephants Dream** because their official project pages state the relevant Creative Commons permissions clearly. Add **Tears of Steel** after a separate review of the credit scroll and intended use. Keep **Cosmos Laundromat** pending exact asset-level licence confirmation.

This is a small technical pilot, not the target launch catalogue. Cinema still needs licensed Ghanaian and African films, series, documentaries and other complete programmes from rights holders and distributors.
