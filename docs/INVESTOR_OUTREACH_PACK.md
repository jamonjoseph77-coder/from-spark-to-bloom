# Project Cinema — Investor Outreach Pack

## Short introduction

Project Cinema is a next-generation television service and digital entertainment home. It brings movies, series, television, live programming, discovery, creator and publisher video, and title-centred conversations into one connected experience.

Cinema is not being positioned as another subscription streaming service or a social-media feed. The business direction is to make viewing accessible and build commercial value around individual content properties through a Digital Box Office: approved title sponsorships, premiere partnerships, licensed windows and B2B distribution.

## What to send before a meeting

- The live website and a short, rehearsed product demonstration.
- A one-page overview with the problem, product, audience, business model and launch plan.
- A 12–18 month budget showing assumptions, milestones and runway.
- A clear list of what works today versus what is still being tested.
- Any real user interviews, pilot evidence or rights-holder conversations, labelled accurately.
- The current US$300,000 pre-seed target and a reviewed use-of-funds budget for product development, rights-cleared content partnerships and Ghana/Nigeria market entry. The target is not secured capital.

Do not send passwords, private customer data, personal identity documents or confidential production credentials in an initial approach.

## Investor follow-up

- Send one concise, individually addressed introduction.
- If there is no response, send one polite follow-up after 5–7 business days.
- If there is still no response after another 7–10 business days, pause and move on to another qualified lead.
- Keep a simple record of the organisation, contact person, date, channel, response and next step.
- Do not interpret silence as a rejection or imply that an investor has reviewed Cinema when they have not replied.

## Important boundaries

Cinema's working website is not proof of signed content rights, paying customers, revenue or a completed payment system. Be precise about these distinctions. Do not promise a film, series, sports stream or broadcaster channel unless the relevant rights are documented.

Before accepting investment, signing agreements or making financial commitments, review the terms with a trusted adult and qualified legal and financial advisers.
